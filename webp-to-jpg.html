When should I convert WEBP to JPG?

Use JPG when a destination does not accept WEBP, when you need predictable compatibility, or when sharing an image with software that expects JPEG.

How to convert WEBP to JPG

Choose WEBP as the source format, choose JPG as the output, then select one or more files. For supported browser conversions, the work happens on your device. Adjust quality when the output format uses lossy image compression, then download each completed result.

Good to know

  • Batch selection is supported.
  • Output names keep the original filename and change the extension.
  • JPG output uses a white background where transparent pixels would otherwise disappear.

Frequently asked questions

Will transparency be preserved?

JPG does not support transparency. This tool uses a white background for transparent areas when creating JPG output.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the browser workflow.

Are my original files uploaded?
