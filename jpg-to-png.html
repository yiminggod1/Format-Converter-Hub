Is JPG to PNG lossless?

The PNG output itself is lossless, but it cannot restore information that was already discarded by the original JPG compression.

How to convert JPG to PNG

Choose JPG as the source format, choose PNG as the output, then select one or more files. For supported browser conversions, the work happens on your device. Adjust quality when the output format uses lossy image compression, then download each completed result.

Good to know

  • Batch selection is supported.
  • Output names keep the original filename and change the extension.
  • JPG output uses a white background where transparent pixels would otherwise disappear.

Frequently asked questions

Will the file become larger?

Usually. PNG often needs more storage than JPG for photographs. Convert when format compatibility or lossless output is the goal rather than minimum file size.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the browser workflow.

Are my original files uploaded?

Supported browser-side conversions are processed locally by the page. HEIC decoding depends on the client-side decoder loading successfully.
