CONVERSION GUIDE

HEIC vs JPG
for everyday sharing.

HEIC is efficient and common on Apple devices. JPG is older, broadly supported, and still a practical interchange format when compatibility matters.

Keep HEIC when size matters

HEIC can be an efficient choice for storing photos on devices and in workflows that already support the format. Keeping the source avoids an unnecessary conversion step.

Use JPG when compatibility matters

JPG is a safer choice for many web forms, older applications, image uploads, and software that does not accept HEIC.

Conversion tips

When converting HEIC to JPG, review the output quality and remember that JPG does not preserve transparency. Our dedicated converter lets you process multiple images and adjust quality.
