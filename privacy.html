Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026.

Files processed by the tools

Supported browser-side conversions are designed to process your selected files in your browser. The original file is not sent to our conversion server for those workflows. Browser compatibility, file size, and decoder availability can affect whether a conversion succeeds.

Third-party resources

The site may load open-source libraries from third-party content delivery networks, including jsDelivr and unpkg. Your browser requests those library files directly from the applicable provider. Those providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, and timestamps according to their own policies.

Advertising and cookies

When Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, local storage, or similar technologies to serve and measure advertising. Google and its partners may use information from your visits to this and other sites to personalize advertising where permitted by your choices and applicable rules.

Google's disclosure about data use on partner sites is available at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can review Google's advertising personalization choices at Google Ads Settings.

Consent and regional choices

Where required, the site will present a privacy or consent message for advertising and related technologies. For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google requires publishers serving personalized ads to use an appropriate certified consent management platform or another supported compliant setup.

Analytics

If analytics tools are enabled in the future, they may collect technical usage information such as pages viewed, device or browser information, and referral information. Any enabled analytics service will be described here.

Data retention

Because supported conversion processing happens in the browser, Format Converter does not need to retain your original files on a conversion server. Browser downloads are controlled by your device and browser settings.

Children

The service is a general-purpose file utility and is not directed specifically at children.
