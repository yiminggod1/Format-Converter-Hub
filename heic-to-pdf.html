Why convert HEIC to PDF?

PDF is convenient for submissions, printing, archiving, and sharing collections of photos. HEIC is efficient for camera storage, but PDF is often the more portable document format.

How to convert HEIC to PDF

Choose HEIC as the source, choose PDF as the target, select one or more files, then let the browser build the output. For image-to-PDF workflows, multiple selected images can become one PDF with one image per page.

Good to know

  • Multiple files can be selected.
  • Page order follows selection order.
  • PDF pages use an A4 layout with margins.
